# 0.4.2 — 2026-11-23

## Added

- **`uniprot_get_entry` `biophysicochemical` section** with Michaelis constants, maximum velocities, pH and temperature dependence, redox potential, and absorption, one object per molecule with evidence ([#47](https://github.com/cyanheads/uniprot-mcp-server/issues/47)). `absorptionNote` sits beside `absorption`; an approximate maximum renders as "(approximate)". `kinetics`, `ph_dependence`, `temp_dependence`, `redox_potential`, and `absorption` join the `fields` vocabulary.
- **`uniprot_get_entry` `residue`** (one accession, canonical numbering) keeps the sites, topology, PTMs, processing, variants, mutagenesis, and domain rows covering that residue, reported in `residueCoverage`; `residue_single_accession` and `residue_out_of_range` reject bad inputs, and window and past-the-end notices keep `residue` in their re-call ([#48](https://github.com/cyanheads/uniprot-mcp-server/issues/48)).
- **`uniprot_get_proteome`** returns `geneCount`, `reviewedProteinCount`, `unreviewedProteinCount`, `isoformProteinCount`, `cpdStatus`, and `exclusionReasons` on excluded proteomes. UniProt's placeholder zeros on non-reference proteomes are omitted ([#49](https://github.com/cyanheads/uniprot-mcp-server/issues/49)).
- **`uniprot_map_ids` `size`** (default 100, served up to 500 with a notice above it) pages a finished job and may accompany `ticket` or `continuation`; results carry `organism_name` ([#50](https://github.com/cyanheads/uniprot-mcp-server/issues/50)).
- **`sequenceFlags`** (Fragment, Fragments, Precursor) on entries and search and proteome hits; search always requests `protein_name`.

## Changed

- **`uniprot_map_ids` `content[]`** renders one table per page in place of the table plus an identity bullet per mapping ([#50](https://github.com/cyanheads/uniprot-mcp-server/issues/50)).
- **`uniprot_map_ids` tickets** poll within `UNIPROT_ID_MAPPING_BUDGET_MS` like a fresh submission, and the whole call ends within 50 seconds. A budget that runs out after the job exists fails `Timeout` naming the job, with its `ticket` or `continuation` in `data`; the job-details read is bounded by `UNIPROT_TIMEOUT_MS` ([#50](https://github.com/cyanheads/uniprot-mcp-server/issues/50)).
- **`uniprot-guide`** skill 1.2 covers `location.isoform`, `residue`, and `size`.

## Fixed

- **`uniprot_search_proteins`** keeps an inactive accession the query names when the default `reviewed` filter or `organism_id` is appended, through an `OR active:false` branch. A query with more OR conditions than UniProt's 100-condition validator accepts skips the branch ([#44](https://github.com/cyanheads/uniprot-mcp-server/issues/44)). A search cursor from 0.4.1 for an affected query fails `invalid_cursor`.
- **`molecule` and per-row `evidence`** on function, catalytic activity, cofactor, and subcellular-location rows; location, cofactor, and ALTERNATIVE PRODUCTS notes as `comments` rows; location `orientation`; isoform `synonyms`; and `functionSnippetMolecule` on search and proteome hits ([#45](https://github.com/cyanheads/uniprot-mcp-server/issues/45)).
- **Feature `location`** carries `startModifier`, `endModifier`, and `isoform`, and positions render in UniProt notation (`a..b`, `<`, `>`, `?n`, `@<isoform>:`) where uncertain, out-of-range, and isoform-numbered positions read as exact canonical ones ([#46](https://github.com/cyanheads/uniprot-mcp-server/issues/46)).
- **`uniprot_map_ids`** a status check that fails upstream after the job exists returns `running` with the ticket, where 0.4.1 lost the job id when this followed a submission; the identity notice names the actual cause instead of always blaming the 100,000-ID limit; an HTML error page from the mapping service comes back as a clean `mapping_failed` message.
- **`uniprot_get_proteome` `not_found`** on a UPID miss no longer claims a reference proteome, and its recovery points to a parent taxon.
